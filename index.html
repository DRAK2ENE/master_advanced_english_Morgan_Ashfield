How this course was built

A methodology grounded
in evidence, not intuition.

Every structural decision — from sequencing to exercise design to annotation style — reflects patterns observed across thousands of real learner interactions.

01
Error Pattern Analysis

The course targets the specific grammatical and lexical errors most frequently observed in B2 writers and speakers — not a generic syllabus, but a precision-mapped curriculum.

02
Corpus-Informed Vocabulary

Every vocabulary item, collocation, and idiom was selected based on frequency data from academic and professional corpora — not intuition or outdated word lists alone.

03
Exam-Aligned Tasks

Exercises mirror the exact formats and cognitive demands of Cambridge C1 Advanced, IELTS Academic, and TOEFL iBT — ensuring that learning transfers directly to performance.

04
Annotated Models

Model essays, passages, and spoken answers are not just presented — they are annotated in detail, making the criteria for success explicit and reproducible.

05
Register Awareness

Every module builds explicit awareness of register — the capacity to match linguistic form to context — which research consistently identifies as the most reliable differentiator of C1 competence.

06
Progressive Complexity

Modules build on each other deliberately — grammatical structures from Module 1 reappear in Module 4 writing tasks, vocabulary from Module 2 surfaces in Module 3 reading passages.
